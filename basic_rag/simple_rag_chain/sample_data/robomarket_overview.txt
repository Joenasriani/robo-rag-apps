RoboMarket Platform Overview
============================

RoboMarket is an AI-powered B2B marketplace that connects buyers and sellers of industrial
robots, collaborative robots (cobots), robotic components, and automation systems. The
platform serves manufacturers, system integrators, distributors, and research institutions
across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

Key Platform Features
---------------------

1. Intelligent Product Catalog
   The catalog contains over 50,000 SKUs spanning six main categories:
   - Industrial robots (6-axis, SCARA, delta, Cartesian)
   - Collaborative robots (cobots) rated for human-robot interaction
   - End-of-arm tooling (grippers, welding torches, vision systems)
   - Motion controllers and servo drives
   - Safety systems (light curtains, safety PLCs, force-torque sensors)
   - Spare parts and consumables

2. AI-Assisted Sourcing
   Buyers submit a specification sheet or natural-language description of their
   automation task. The platform's RAG-powered sourcing engine matches requirements to
   products, retrieves relevant technical datasheets, and generates a ranked shortlist
   with justification drawn directly from supplier documentation.

3. Supplier Verification
   All suppliers undergo a three-stage verification: legal entity check, product
   authenticity audit, and reference customer validation. Verified suppliers receive a
   trust badge visible on their storefront.

4. RFQ and Negotiation Workflow
   Qualified buyers can send Requests for Quotation (RFQs) directly through the platform.
   AI-drafted RFQ templates pre-fill technical requirements extracted from the product
   catalog. Suppliers respond within 72 hours or lose prioritization in search rankings.

5. Integration APIs
   RoboMarket exposes REST and GraphQL APIs for:
   - Product catalog search and filtering
   - Real-time inventory and lead-time data
   - Order management and shipment tracking
   - Supplier rating and review aggregation

Pricing Model
-------------
RoboMarket operates on a transaction-fee model. Buyers pay no subscription fee.
Suppliers pay a tiered listing fee based on catalog size and a 1.5–3.5% transaction
fee on completed orders. Enterprise buyers with annual spend above $1M USD qualify
for negotiated platform rates and dedicated account management.

Data and Privacy
----------------
All transactional data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
RoboMarket does not sell supplier or buyer data to third parties. Aggregated,
anonymized market intelligence reports are available to premium subscribers.

Support and SLA
---------------
Platform uptime SLA is 99.9% (three nines). Technical support is available 24/7
for enterprise accounts and during business hours (UTC 08:00–20:00) for standard
accounts. Dedicated implementation engineers are assigned to deployments exceeding
$500K in projected annual transaction volume.
